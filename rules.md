# Keystone Rules

These are your personal commitments. The app does not enforce them. Your honesty does. Edit each rule to match what you actually intend, not what sounds good. Re-read this when you're tempted to fudge.

The reset rule: if you miss the SAME habit two days in a row, the streak resets to zero. Different habits missed on consecutive days are forgiven. Today is in progress and never counts as a miss.

---

## 1. Read 10 pages
Physical book or ebook. Audiobooks do NOT count. 10 full pages, in one sitting or split across the day.

## 2. Weigh in
Step on the scale. Record the number in the app. No avoiding it on bad days.

## 3. 64oz water
Sixty-four fluid ounces of plain water. Coffee, tea, sparkling water, juice, alcohol do not count toward the total.

## 4. 45 min workout
Forty-five minutes of intentional movement at intensity. Walking counts only at brisk pace (3.5+ mph). Stretching and yoga count if heart rate is elevated.

## 5. Compliant meal (OMAD / Keto / Whole foods)
**EDIT THIS.** Pick one approach or define your own. Examples:
- OMAD: one meal per day, no calories outside the eating window
- Keto: under 30g net carbs for the day
- Whole foods: nothing processed, nothing with ingredients you cannot pronounce
- Or: a single combined definition you commit to in writing

## 6. Visualization
Ten minutes minimum. Eyes closed. Future-self specific scene. Repeated visualization, not a new one each day.

## 7. Social food/drinks/snacks only
**EDIT THIS.** Clarify what you actually mean. Three readings:
- Reading A: only consume snacks/drinks in social settings (no solo snacking at home)
- Reading B: outside your meal, only social-occasion items count
- Reading C: something else specific to you

## 8. Cold shower
Three minutes minimum at the cold setting. Full body. A cold finish on a normal shower does not count, must be the whole shower.

---

## When in doubt
If you're asking yourself "does this count?", it doesn't.
