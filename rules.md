# Keystone Rules

These are your personal commitments. The app does not enforce them. Your honesty does. Re-read this when you're tempted to fudge.

The reset rule: if you miss the SAME habit two days in a row, the streak resets to zero. Different habits missed on consecutive days are forgiven. Today is in progress and never counts as a miss.

---

## 1. Read 10 pages
Physical book or ebook. 10 full pages, in one sitting or split across the day.

## 2. Weigh in
Step on the scale. Record the number in the app. No avoiding it on bad days.

## 3. 64oz water
Sixty-four fluid ounces of plain water. Coffee, tea, sparkling water, juice, alcohol do not count toward the total. More as needed.

## 4. 45 min workout
Forty-five minutes of intentional movement. Walking counts. Stretching and yoga count.

## 5. Compliant meal (OMAD / Keto / Whole foods)
- OMAD: one meal per day, no calories outside the eating window
- Keto: under 30g net carbs for the day
- Whole foods: nothing processed, nothing with ingredients you cannot pronounce.

## 6. Visualization
Ten minutes minimum. Prefer the Psychocybernetics version.

## 7. Social food/drinks/snacks only
- No cheat meals or alchohol except in social situations.

## 8. Cold shower
Full body. A cold finish on a normal shower counts
